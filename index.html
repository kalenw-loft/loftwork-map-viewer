How to read and move

Where a dot sits. Each column is a society domain. It branches by Loftwork's own theme tags, and height is the year.

How big it is. Its weight: how often other pages link to it, whether it grew sub-pages, how many people are credited, and how long it is. Being translated adds a little, but a well-cited Japanese-only piece still outweighs a translated one.

Bases. Programmes and places Loftwork started that later work builds on (100BANCH, AWRD, YouFab, MTRL…). Each is labelled where it started; pick one to see only its pieces, with lines from the start to everything that grew from it.

Languages. The map shows Japanese content. "Languages" (top right) colours each dot by which versions exist: Japanese only, also in English, also in Traditional Chinese only.

Lines. Faint lines are page links. Strands between columns show how much work the two domains share. Click a dot to see what it links to, work for the same client, and work by the same people.

Trackpad. Two fingers sideways to orbit, up and down to move through the years, pinch to zoom. Click and drag also orbits; Shift + two fingers pans.

Mouse. Scroll wheel zooms toward the cursor; drag to orbit. On a trackpad, hold ⌘ while scrolling to zoom the same way.
